about

i'm Shaurya — a data scientist at Skan.AI and an electrical engineering grad from IIT Delhi. i work mostly on LLMs and agents: right now i lead a small team building a computer-use agent that drives desktop GUIs for BFSI workflows, and before that i built process-discovery pipelines with fine-tuned models and multi-model RAG.

the stuff i genuinely enjoy sits closer to the metal — making models reason better and run cheaper. i've written custom Triton GPU kernels for memory-efficient training (fused linear+cross-entropy, RMSNorm, SwiGLU, RoPE), played with parallel latent reasoning, and dabbled in cryptography for fun (homomorphic encryption, a toy cryptocurrency).

this site is where i think out loud: what i build, what breaks, and what i get wrong. lately that's meant open source, efficient training, and inference.

you can find me on GitHub, read my CV, or reach out at shaurya.m207@gmail.com.
