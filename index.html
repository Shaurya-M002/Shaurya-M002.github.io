About

who i am and what i work on.

i'm Shaurya — a Data Scientist at Skan.AI and an electrical engineering grad from IIT Delhi. right now i lead a small team building a computer-use agent that drives desktop GUIs for BFSI workflows: a stateless step executor driven by an agentic orchestration procedure, with set-of-mark prompting and a thinker-grounder split for visual grounding.

before that i built process-discovery pipelines — next-activity prediction with a custom transformer framework, fine-tuned LLMs (Qwen, Flan-T5, Gemma), and multi-model RAG for contextual reasoning and action planning.

the part i genuinely enjoy sits closer to the metal: making models reason better and run cheaper. i've written custom Triton GPU kernels for memory-efficient training, explored parallel latent reasoning, and dabble in cryptography for fun.

Projects

things i built to understand something better.

PaCT

Parallel Continuous Thought for LLM Reasoning

A parallel latent-reasoning framework that removes recurrent unrolling in continuous thought via stage-wise parallel latent resynthesis.

  • Learnable query codebook + cross-attention, bidirectional within-stage refinement.
  • 4–9× training speedup over Coconut, matching/beating accuracy on GSM8k, ProntoQA, ProsQA; scaled to LLaMA-3B/8B.

Forge

Custom GPU Kernels for LLM Training

Custom Triton kernels for memory-efficient training.

  • Fused linear + cross-entropy with tiled vocab, avoiding full logit materialization.
  • Single-pass fused RMSNorm/LayerNorm; custom SwiGLU and RoPE with fused fwd/bwd.
